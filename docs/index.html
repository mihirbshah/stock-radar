About these signals

Breakout candidates have a 1-month return above 15%, a 3-month return above 30%, latest daily volume greater than 1.5 times the previous 20 sessions' average, and a price above both the 50-day and 200-day moving averages.

The 0–100 score combines momentum, volume and trend strength. A 3-month gain above 100% is flagged as extended. These scores are not future-return probabilities, buy recommendations or financial advice.

This free version scans only the symbols in the repository's tickers.txt, not the full US stock market. It does not yet analyze earnings, news, analyst estimates, market capitalization, institutional activity, or Reddit sentiment. Free Stooq data may be delayed, unavailable or adjusted.
